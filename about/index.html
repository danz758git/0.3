About us

Named for the view from above

The pterosaur was the first vertebrate to take flight — and the first to see the landscape whole. That’s the vantage point we bring to enterprise technology.

Why we exist

Systems fail in the gaps between vendors

The network team blames the software. The software team blames the infrastructure. The infrastructure was installed by a contractor nobody can reach. Every organization knows this story — and every chapter of it happens in the seams between specialists.

Apex Integration was founded to own the seams. We work across the full stack of enterprise technology — structured cabling and data center builds, resilient multi-path networking, custom software, applied AI, on-site video surveillance, and operational digital twins — so that when we hand over a system, it behaves like one system. Our clients get a single accountable partner with the altitude to see how every layer affects every other.

We build on field-proven platforms from world-class technology partners, and we hold certifications that keep our engineering current. But the thing we’re proudest of is simpler: the phone number our clients call is the same one that answers.
